Happy birthday, Doris. Heri ya kuzaliwa, dada yangu.

Some people come into our lives as friends and slowly become family. That’s what you are to me. I’m grateful for your place in my life, for the person you are, and for all the light you bring simply by being yourself.

I love seeing the different sides of you in these photos: the playful arcade moments, your pose in front of the music wall, and the quieter moments too. I hope the year ahead brings you many more reasons to smile.

Today, I hope you feel celebrated in every way. I hope you laugh loudly, feel deeply loved, eat something delicious, and make a memory that makes you smile long after your birthday is over.

As you step into twenty-two, I’m wishing you peace when life feels busy, courage for every new step, joy in the little things, and beautiful opportunities that remind you how much you can do. May the things you’ve been hoping for begin finding their way to you.

Thank you for being someone I can call a friend and love like a sister. I’ll always be proud of you, rooting for you, and wishing good things for you.

Happy birthday, Doris. I love you like a sister. May this be a wonderful year for you.

With love,
Benjamin ♡
