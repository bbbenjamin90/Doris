Doris Birthday Website

PREVIEW
Keep every file in this folder together, then open index.html in a modern browser. Photos and the birthday video are local files. Music and web fonts need an internet connection.

SHARE THE SITE
Upload the whole folder to a static web host, then send Doris the hosted link. The opening passcode is 2510 (day and month of the birthday). It is a playful reveal, not security; the code is visible in the JavaScript. The passcode and a 5-second countdown appear on every visit.

IMPORTANT BEFORE SHARING ON WHATSAPP
In index.html, change the og:image line from content="og-preview.jpg" to the full address, e.g. https://YOUR-SITE.com/og-preview.jpg, so the link preview shows a photo.

MUSIC (Paul Clement, on Spotify)
Playlist order: Unaweza, Nafasi, Mwokozi, Safari, then it repeats. The first song starts when she unlocks the page; each next song starts when the last ends.
FULL SONGS: Spotify plays full songs inside a web page only for people logged in to Spotify (Premium). Everyone else gets 30-second previews, and the page says so with a login link.
IF THERE IS NO SOUND: browsers block automatic sound, so the page shows the Spotify player and asks her to tap the play button once. If Spotify's script is blocked or too slow, the page switches by itself to a plain Spotify player she can tap. Always test on the hosted link, not a double-clicked file, and with phone volume up and silent mode off.

Her note opens an email draft to lor.adia.17@gmail.com that she must send herself. If no email app opens, "Copy my note instead" copies the note and address.

PRIVACY
Favorites and saved wishes stay in the visitor's browser. Google Fonts, Spotify, and YouTube are external services.

IF YOU CAN'T HEAR THE SONG: Spotify blocks automatic sound in many browsers. Tap the play button inside the Spotify player once (the page shows it until music starts). Test on the hosted link, not by double-clicking the file.

SEE HOW SHE RESPONDS (about 3 minutes to set up)
1. Go to web3forms.com, choose "Create your Access Key", enter lor.adia.17@gmail.com, and copy the key they email you.
2. Open doris.js, find the line   const REPORTING = { accessKey: '' };   and paste the key between the quotes.
3. Upload the site. Then open your site address followed by #benjamin (for example https://yoursite.com/#benjamin) and press "Send a test report". Check your inbox and spam folder.
WHAT YOU RECEIVE (by email): a short note when she has opened the site, another every few minutes while she explores, and one when she leaves or switches apps. Each lists what she did (stars found, letters opened, cards flipped, candles blown, game finished, wheel prize, music played, how far she scrolled, time on the page). When she picks a reaction emoji and presses Send, her note arrives straight in your inbox.
PRIVACY BUILT IN: once the key is set, the opening screen tells her you will see which surprises she opens, and gives her a switch to turn sharing off. Photos, the lantern wishes she types and her sealed time-capsule note are never sent. The free plan has monthly limits.
Until the key is added, nothing is sent and the notice stays hidden; her note falls back to an email draft.

KEEP IT & SHARE IT
Near the end, "Make it yours to keep" shows her birthday ticket as a 9:16 picture. "Share my ticket" opens her phone's share sheet (WhatsApp Status etc.); if the phone cannot share pictures, it saves the picture instead. "Add to my home screen" installs the page like an app (manifest.webmanifest and the icon files must stay in the folder). These need the site to be on a web address (https); they do not work from a double-clicked file.
